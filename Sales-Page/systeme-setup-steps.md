# Setting up Scrolling to Selling in Systeme.io (step by step)

Menu names in Systeme.io change now and then. If something looks different, look for the closest match, or check Systeme's help center.

## What you need before you start
- Your Systeme.io account (sign up through your affiliate link)
- `Product/Scrolling-to-Selling-v2.pdf` (your product)
- `Sales-Page/sales-copy.md` (your copy, ready to paste)
- Colors: sage #6F8266 · deep sage #3E4E38 · soft blush #F6DCD8 · cream #F7EFE8
- A Stripe or PayPal account to get paid

## Part 1: Set up the basics
1. Log in. In **Settings**, add your business name (Grit & Bloom) and a sender email address for the emails your buyers receive.
2. Under **Settings > Payment** (or similar), connect **Stripe** and/or **PayPal**. Follow the prompts.
3. Optional: connect a custom domain later. The free Systeme address works to start.

## Part 2: Create the product
1. Go to **Sell > Products** (or **Products**) and click **Create**.
2. Name: Scrolling to Selling. Type: digital / one-time payment. Price: **$37**.
3. Save it.

## Part 3: Build the funnel
1. Go to **Funnels > Create**. Choose a **Sales funnel** and give it a name.
2. The funnel has steps, usually: **Sales page > Order form > Thank-you page**. Keep those three.

## Part 4: Build the sales page
1. Open the **Sales page** step and choose a **blank** or simple template.
2. Rebuild the page section by section using the editor's blocks, pasting copy from `sales-copy.md` in this order:
   Headline and subhead > The problem > The turn (your story) > What you'll be able to do > Is this for you? > Everything included and price > FAQ > Final call to action.
3. Use the colors above. Use a serif font for headlines and a clean sans-serif for body text.
4. Add a button in the hero and near the price. Set each button to go to the **Order form** step.
5. Add the guarantee text under the price (copy it from `sales-copy.md`, section "Price + guarantee").
6. Check the **mobile view** in the editor. Most visitors will be on phones.
7. Add footer links to your **Privacy Policy**, **Terms**, and **Refund Policy** pages (see Part 8).

## Part 5: Set up the order form
1. Open the **Order form** step. Add your **Scrolling to Selling** product at $37.
2. Keep the form short: name and email.
3. Later, this is where the Reel Vault order bump goes.

## Part 6: Thank-you page and delivery
1. Open the **Thank-you page** step. Add a headline like "You're in! Here's your guide."
2. Upload the PDF (in the file or media area) and add a **Download** button linking to it.
3. Also send the file by email: create an **Automation rule** (or workflow) with the trigger "Purchased Scrolling to Selling" and the action "Send email" containing the download link.
4. Add a **tag** (for example, "Buyer: Scrolling to Selling") to everyone who buys so you can email them later.

## Part 7: Test like a buyer
1. Make a **100% discount coupon** (or use test mode) and buy your own product.
2. Confirm the thank-you page works, the email arrives, and the download opens on your phone.
3. Fix anything that's off, then delete the test contact and coupon.

## Part 8: Pages you need before running ads
Create simple **Privacy Policy**, **Terms of Service** and **Refund Policy** pages and link them in your footer. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and payment processors expect them. Ask me and I'll draft the text, and have a lawyer review anything you're unsure about.

## Part 9: Publish
1. Publish the funnel.
2. Copy the **sales page link**. This is the link your ads and content will point to.
3. Send me a screenshot if anything looks off.
