AI Freedom Machine - Auto-Pilot Edition
=======================================

Welcome! Setup is four short steps. Your assistant does the technical parts.


FIRST - the files in here look technical. Ignore them.
------------------------------------------------------
You do NOT need to open, edit, or understand any of these:

  system          <- your automation engine. Your assistant uses it.
  .env.template   <- a blank form for your keys. The connect page fills it in.
  .claude         <- your 14 agents, already installed.
  GET-STARTED.md  <- your assistant reads this to orient itself.
  UPDATING.md     <- how your assistant installs a new version for you, right
                     here in this folder. You just say "let's get updated".
  EDITION.txt and CLAUDE.md  <- two tiny notes that tell your assistant which
                                edition this is.

(Product and Content are plain folders for your finished product and your
 content. Your assistant fills them. Product has a short README inside; Content's
 is in Content/ads.)

(On a Mac, .env.template and .claude are hidden, so you won't see them at all.
 That's normal. On Windows they may be hidden too.)


STEP 1 - Put this folder somewhere permanent
--------------------------------------------
  Windows: move the "AI Freedom Machine - Auto-Pilot" folder into Documents.
           Use the INNER folder - the one with this READ ME FIRST file directly
           inside it. ("Extract All" can put it inside a second folder with the
           same name; that outer one is just a wrapper.)
  Mac:     Safari usually unzips the download for you, so the folder is already
           in Downloads (the .zip goes to the Trash; that's normal). If you only
           see the .zip, double-click it. Then move the folder into your home
           folder (the one with your name - NOT Desktop, Documents or Downloads;
           macOS blocks background jobs there). To open your home folder: in
           Finder choose Go > Home from the menu bar (or press Shift + Cmd + H),
           then drag the folder in.
Don't rename or move it later - if you ever do, just tell your assistant.


STEP 2 - Open the Claude desktop app, then the CODE tab
-------------------------------------------------------
Get it from claude.ai/download if you haven't already, and sign in (paid plan -
Pro is plenty). Mac: macOS 13.5 (Ventura) or newer - check Apple menu > About
This Mac. Click the Code tab, then choose this folder (the inner one, with
READ ME FIRST directly inside) as your project.

  Windows: if it asks you to install Git, say yes (or ask your assistant how).
  Mac:     if a pop-up says 'The "git" command requires the command line
           developer tools', click Install (free, from Apple, a few minutes).
           It's the Mac version of the Windows Git step, and it may pop up
           again once or twice while it downloads; that's normal.


STEP 3 - Say hello
------------------
Type:

    let's get started

The first time, your assistant installs its engine for you (it takes a few
minutes), runs a quick health check so you can see what's connected, then asks
what hours your computer is usually on and you're logged in, so automations
only run when they'll actually work.

  Windows: it may ask to install Node.js - say yes. If Windows asks
           "allow changes?", click Yes.
  Mac:     your assistant sends you to nodejs.org. Click the green LTS button,
           open the downloaded .pkg, click Continue until it asks for your Mac
           password (you need an admin account for this one step), then
           restart the Claude app.


STEP 4 - Connect your accounts
------------------------------
Your assistant opens a small "connect page" in your browser. You paste each key
into its card and click "Save & test" - one at a time, with exact directions for where to
find each one. Your keys stay on your computer.


WHAT YOU'LL SEE
---------------
It will ask permission before running things or saving files. That's normal -
nothing happens on your computer without your OK.

  Mac: after your automations are turned on, your Mac shows a notice:
       "Background Items Added - software from 'Node.js Foundation' added
       items that can run in the background". Nothing on screen says AI
       Freedom Machine; that's your Machine registering its schedule. Leave
       the switch for Node.js Foundation ON (it may show as node or Unknown
       Developer). If it's ever off, your automations stop - turn it back on
       in System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions (called just
       Login Items on macOS 13 and 14). It's one switch for all your automations
       (and anything else on your Mac that runs through Node.js), so turning
       it off stops all of them at once.

  Getting Started guide  - these steps in more detail, plus the connect page card by card
  What It Can Do         - every agent and every automation. Read it after setup.
