AI Freedom Machine - Auto-Pilot Edition
=======================================

Welcome! Everything you need is in this folder. Start here, then follow the guide.


FIRST - a word about the files you can see
------------------------------------------
Some of these look technical. You do NOT need to open, edit, or understand any of them:

  system            <- your automation engine. Your assistant uses it. Leave it alone.
  .env.template     <- a blank form for your own passwords, filled in later WITH you.
  .claude           <- your 14 specialists, already installed. You may not even see this
                       folder; that's normal, it's hidden by default.

Nothing here is broken and nothing is missing. You only ever touch the two PDFs.


STEP 1 - Read the guide
-----------------------
Open "AI Freedom Machine - Auto-Pilot - Getting Started.pdf".

It walks you through the setup click by click: creating your Claude account, installing the
free engine (Node.js), installing your assistant, and opening it inside this folder.

Give it 15-20 minutes for the first run. It is more involved than the desktop version -
that's the honest trade for something that afterwards runs without you. None of it needs
tech skills, and every step is spelled out.

  (The other PDF - "What It Can Do" - lists every capability, every command, and every
   automation that will run for you. Read that AFTER you're set up. It's a map, not
   homework.)


STEP 2 - Say hello
------------------
With your assistant open in this folder, type:

    let's get started

It will ask what hours your computer is usually on, then take it from there.


IF ANYTHING GOES SIDEWAYS
-------------------------
Three fixes cover almost every install snag, and all three take under a minute:

  "node is not recognized"     -> close and reopen your command window
  "npm install failed"         -> make sure you're inside this folder, then run it again
  "claude command not found"   -> run the install line once more, then reopen the window

Still stuck? Two options, in this order:
  1. Search the Help Vault:  helpvault.theaifreedommachine.com
  2. Reply to your welcome email. It comes straight to me and I answer personally,
     usually within one business day. Send a screenshot if it's easier.

Please don't try to do all of this tonight. The people who get stuck are almost always the
ones who sat down determined to finish in one sitting, hit a snag at 11pm, and closed the
laptop. One step at a time is genuinely faster.
