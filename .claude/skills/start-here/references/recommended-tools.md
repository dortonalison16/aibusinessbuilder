# Recommended Tools (with setup links)

*Shared reference — every agent carries this file. These are the tools Ash, the founder of AI
Systems Method, recommends for this kind of business. Some links are affiliate links: if the owner
signs up through one, AI Systems Method may earn a small commission at no extra cost to them. That's
why the disclosure rule below is not optional.*

## When to recommend (the only rule that matters)

Recommend a tool **only when it's genuinely the next thing the owner needs** for the job in front of
you — never as padding, never as a list, never twice in a row. One tool, one sentence, free tier
mentioned, always skippable. If they already use something that does the job, support that instead,
warmly. Being useful is what earns the click.

## How to present a link

- Hide the raw URL behind a word: `You can set up your Metricool account [here](https://f.mtr.cool/MZQGFM).`
- **Disclose, every time an affiliate link appears** (marked ★ below), right next to it. First time
  in a conversation, use the full line; after that the short form is fine:
  - Full: *(Affiliate link: AI Systems Method may earn a small commission if you sign up, at no extra cost to you.)*
  - Short: *(affiliate link)*
- Never alter or invent a link. If a tool isn't listed, link its official homepage.
- Never share Ash's API keys, passwords or logins. These are **signup** links so the owner creates
  their **own** account.
- **Never put these links inside the owner's own product, sales page, emails or posts.** Those are
  the owner's business and carry *their* links (from their `affiliate-links.md`) or none at all.

## Tools to be careful with

- Don't recommend a paid plan when the free tier covers what they need today.
- Don't recommend a tool to fix a problem that isn't the bottleneck (e.g. social-proof pop-ups with
  zero traffic). Say what the real bottleneck is instead.
- Don't make claims about a tool's features you can't verify. Describe what it does in plain terms.
