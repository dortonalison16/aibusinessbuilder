# Chart of Accounts — Starter (for a digital / online business)
*A sensible, generic starting point the Finance Agent customizes to the user's business and country.
This is a template, NOT anyone's real books. Categories and tax treatment vary by country and
business structure — always confirm with the user's accountant.*

## How to use it
Start here, then trim/rename to fit the user. Fewer, clearer categories beat a long list nobody
maintains. The goal: every transaction has an obvious home, and the P&L tells a clear story.

---

## INCOME
- **Product Sales** — your digital products / courses
- **Affiliate Income** — commissions from tools you recommend
- **Service Income** — any done-for-you / coaching / freelance work
- **Other Income** — refunds received, misc.

## COST OF SALES (directly tied to delivering/selling)
- **Payment Processing Fees** — Stripe / PayPal / processor fees
- **Platform / Marketplace Fees** — Gumroad, Etsy, app store cuts
- **Refunds & Chargebacks** — money returned to customers

## OPERATING EXPENSES
- **Software & Subscriptions** — your tool stack (AI tools, funnel/email software, design tools)
- **Advertising & Marketing** — ad spend (Meta, Google), promo costs
- **Contractors & Freelancers** — VAs, editors, designers you pay
- **Website & Hosting** — domains, hosting, plugins
- **Education & Training** — courses, books, memberships that grow the business
- **Office Supplies & Software** — small tools, apps, stationery
- **Equipment & Hardware** — laptop, mic, camera (may be an asset, not an expense — ask accountant)
- **Home Office** — the business-use portion of rent/utilities/internet (rules vary — confirm)
- **Bank & Finance Charges** — bank fees, currency conversion, interest
- **Professional Fees** — accounting, bookkeeping, legal
- **Travel** — business travel (rules vary by country)
- **Meals & Entertainment** — often only *partly* deductible — flag for the accountant
- **Taxes, Licenses & Permits** — business registration, filing fees
- **Miscellaneous / Other** — the catch-all (keep it small)

## NOT income or expense (don't miscount these — ask the accountant)
- **Owner's Pay / Draws** — money you take out (often not a business "expense")
- **Sales Tax / VAT / GST collected** — money you hold for the government, not your income
- **Loans / Capital In** — money lent to or invested in the business
- **Asset Purchases** — big items that get depreciated, not expensed all at once

---

> Tax treatment (what's deductible, how much, depreciation, sales-tax obligations) **depends on the
> user's country and business structure** — the Finance Agent always pairs this with the disclaimer
> and points anything that affects filing to a qualified professional.
